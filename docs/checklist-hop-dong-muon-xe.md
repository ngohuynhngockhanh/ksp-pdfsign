# Checklist hop dong muon xe ca nhan cho cong ty

## Muc tieu da thong nhat

- Ca nhan cho cong ty muon xe, khong thu tien thue co dinh.
- Xe duoc dung cho viec gap go, don tiep khach hang, khao sat, trien khai va chot hop dong.
- Cong ty chi thanh toan chi phi thuc te phat sinh khi dung xe cho cong viec, vi du xang dau khoang 500.000 dong/luc va bao duong dinh ky.
- Khong dua chi phi su dung ca nhan, tien phat giao thong, do xe hoac khau hao xe vao chi phi cong ty.
- Hop dong can ghi ro cong ty chiu cac chi phi van hanh hop ly trong thoi gian muon xe.

## Cau hoi can tra loi de soan hop dong

### 1. Thong tin ben cho muon

1. Ho va ten chu xe?
2. So CCCD, ngay cap va noi cap?
3. Dia chi thuong tru?
4. So dien thoai?
5. Chu xe co dong thoi la giam doc, chu so huu hoac thanh vien gop von cua cong ty khong?

### 2. Thong tin cong ty muon xe

1. Ten phap ly day du cua cong ty?
2. Ma so thue?
3. Dia chi tru so?
4. Nguoi dai dien theo phap luat va chuc vu?
5. Ai se ky hop dong thay mat cong ty neu chu xe cung la nguoi dai dien theo phap luat?

### 3. Thong tin xe

1. Loai xe, hang xe va dong xe?
2. Bien kiem soat?
3. Mau son?
4. So khung va so may?
5. So dang ky xe, ngay cap va noi cap?
6. Nam san xuat?
7. Tinh trang xe khi ban giao?
8. So kilomet tai thoi diem ban giao?

### 4. Thoi han va pham vi su dung

1. Ngay bat dau cho muon?
2. Thoi han co dinh hay den khi mot ben thong bao cham dut?
3. Can bao truoc bao nhieu ngay khi muon cham dut?
4. Ai duoc phep lai xe?
5. Xe duoc su dung trong tinh, toan quoc hay co gioi han khu vuc?
6. Xe co tiep tuc duoc dung cho muc dich ca nhan khong?
7. Neu dung lan ca nhan va cong ty, cach tach chi phi nhu the nao?

### 5. Chi phi cong ty duoc phep thanh toan

Danh dau tung khoan muon ghi vao hop dong:

- Xang dau phuc vu chuyen di cong viec.
- Phi ETC, cau duong va pha.
- Phi gui xe.
- Rua xe, ve sinh xe.
- Bao duong dinh ky.
- Thay dau, loc dau va vat tu tieu hao.
- Lop xe, acquy va sua chua thong thuong.
- Dang kiem, phi su dung duong bo.
- Bao hiem bat buoc, bao hiem than vo.
- Khoan khac can thoa thuan cu the.

Voi tung nhom chi phi, can xac dinh:

1. Cong ty thanh toan toan bo hay chi phan phuc vu kinh doanh?
2. Muc gioi han moi lan hoac moi thang?
3. Khoan nao can phe duyet truoc?
4. Ai la nguoi phe duyet?

### 6. Chung tu va quy trinh thanh toan

1. Cong ty se dung tai khoan ngan hang, the cong ty hay tam ung cho nguoi lai xe?
2. Thong tin ten cong ty va ma so thue de xuat hoa don?
3. Co su dung tai khoan ETC rieng cho xe khong?
4. Ai giu hoa don va lap bang ke hanh trinh?
5. Cong ty muon theo doi hanh trinh theo tung chuyen hay tong hop theo thang?
6. Cac thong tin toi thieu tren nhat trinh: ngay, noi di/noi den, khach hang, muc dich, kilomet dau/cuoi va chi phi.

### 7. Tai nan, vi pham va sua chua lon

1. Ai chiu tien phat giao thong? Mac dinh nen la nguoi vi pham, khong hach toan vao chi phi cong ty.
2. Ai chiu muc mien thuong bao hiem khi xay ra tai nan?
3. Sua chua lon tu muc bao nhieu tien phai co chu xe dong y bang van ban?
4. Xu ly the nao neu xe hu hong khi dang phuc vu cong viec?
5. Xu ly the nao neu xe hu hong trong luc su dung ca nhan?

### 8. Ban giao va ho so kem theo

1. Co lap bien ban giao nhan xe rieng khong?
2. Ban sao dang ky xe, dang kiem va bao hiem con hieu luc?
3. Danh sach phu kien, chia khoa va giay to giao kem?
4. Co can quyet dinh cua chu so huu/hoi dong thanh vien phe duyet giao dich khong?

## Bo ho so de nghi luu tai cong ty

1. Hop dong muon xe khong thu tien.
2. Bien ban giao nhan xe.
3. Ban sao dang ky xe, dang kiem va bao hiem.
4. Quyet dinh phe duyet tiep nhan va su dung xe.
5. Quy che su dung xe va dinh muc xang dau ngan gon.
6. Bang theo doi hanh trinh theo thang.
7. Hoa don, chung tu thanh toan va phieu bao duong/sua chua.
8. Tai lieu lien quan den chuyen di: lich hen, bao gia, hop dong hoac bien ban lam viec neu co.

## Nguyen tac an toan khi hach toan

- Hoa don nen mang ten va ma so thue cong ty.
- Uu tien cong ty thanh toan truc tiep cho nha cung cap.
- Khoan tu 5 trieu dong tro len, bao gom VAT, nen thanh toan khong dung tien mat.
- Muc chi phai phu hop voi tan suat va quang duong di cong viec.
- Bao duong, sua chua lon can ho so chat hon chi phi xang dau thong thuong.
- Khong hach toan khau hao vi xe khong thuoc so huu cong ty.
- Khong hach toan tien phat, chuyen di rieng hoac chi phi nang cap mang tinh ca nhan.
- Neu cong ty tra mot khoan co dinh cho chu xe, can danh gia lai nguy co bi xem la hop dong thue xe.

## Cach khoi dong lai cong viec lan sau

Nguoi dung co the noi: "Mo checklist hop dong muon xe va hoi toi tung cau". Tro ly se hoi lan luot theo cac muc tren, tong hop cau tra loi, sau do soan hop dong va cac bieu mau kem theo.
